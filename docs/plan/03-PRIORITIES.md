## Ordering notes

1. **Phase 1 is evidence and reliability.** Ranks 1–10 close the gap between present unit/image checks and the user-visible installation, chooser, update, recovery and hardware claims. The owner’s release evidence rules remain: CI, unit, VM, Bazzite backend, NebulaOS image and hardware results are distinct.
2. **Phase 2 begins when core interfaces are stable.** Start with read-only Control Center views over existing tested data; add privileged actions only after an explicit D-Bus allowlist and polkit design. Do not move the GUI to root to simplify implementation.
3. **Gaming and display tuning are evidence-led.** The comparison warns against transplanting schedulers or claiming gains without matching hardware and repeatable benchmarks. No automatic overclocking, power limit, scheduler or GPU change is justified by detection alone.
4. **Retain Bazzite’s platform.** Do not build a new kernel/driver stack, a general package store, or multiple images without demonstrated need. Preserve the two-experience chooser and keep AI tools out of scope.
